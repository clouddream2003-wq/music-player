This Privacy Policy explains how LRC-Maker & Editor handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to work primarily on your device for lyrics editing, but it uses third-party advertising services that may collect information as described below.

1. Overview

LRC-Maker & Editor allows you to create, edit, synchronize, preview, and save .lrc lyrics files for songs stored on your device. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize data collection.

This policy describes what information is accessed through device permissions, what information may be collected by third-party advertising partners, and how you can manage your privacy choices via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

2. Information We Collect and Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Information accessed locally on your device (not transmitted to our servers)

  • Audio file metadata: When you grant media permission, the app reads information about audio files on your device such as title, artist, album, and file location to help you select tracks for lyrics synchronization. The audio content itself is not uploaded to us.
  • User-generated lyrics content: Lyrics text you type or edit and timestamps you create. These are stored as .lrc files in your chosen storage location on your device.
  • App preferences: Settings such as last used folder, UI preferences, and playback state, stored locally via on-device storage.

Information we do not directly collect via our own servers

  • We do not operate our own server to collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location from within the app interface.
  • We do not require account creation and do not collect login credentials.
  • We do not upload your audio files or your .lrc files to our servers.

Information collected by third-party partners

Third-party advertising services integrated in the app, specifically Google AdMob, may automatically collect certain information from your device to provide and measure ads, as described in Section 4. This may include device identifiers, IP address, advertising ID, and interaction with ads.

3. Audio-File Access, Storage Permissions, and User-Generated Lyrics

To function as an LRC editor, the app needs access to your device storage:

  • Android 13 and above: READ_MEDIA_AUDIO to discover and read audio files you choose to work with.
  • Android 12 and below: READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and where applicable WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or Storage Access Framework folder access to browse audio files and save .lrc files.

How this data is used:

  • To display a list of audio tracks available on your device so you can select one for synchronization.
  • To extract album artwork locally for preview purposes using on-device decoding.
  • To save .lrc files you create to the location you select. You control where files are saved and you can delete them at any time via the app or your file manager.

Your lyrics and timing data remain under your control. The app does not transmit your lyrics to us or to third parties for lyrics storage purposes. If you share an .lrc file using system share features, that sharing is performed by you through the Android system.

4. Advertising Services and Third-Party Data Collection

The app displays advertisements to support development. The integrated advertising provider is:

  • Google AdMob - provides banner ads, interstitial ads (shown for example after saving), and app open ads. Ad unit IDs are configured in the app and served by Google.

AdMob may collect and use:

  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, and language settings.
  • Advertising identifiers such as Google Advertising ID.
  • IP address, which may be used to infer general location (e.g., city-level) and for fraud prevention.
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether you viewed or clicked an ad, and performance data.
  • Other information as described in Google's privacy policy and AdMob documentation.

Ad personalization may depend on your device settings and on the consent choices you provide via Google's User Messaging Platform where applicable.

Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control the data collection practices of Google AdMob. You can learn more at: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

5. User Consent and Privacy Choices Through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle consent requirements in certain regions, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

How it works:

  • When you first open the app, or when required by applicable law, the app requests consent information from Google's UMP.
  • If Google determines that a consent form is required for your region, the app will display a consent form provided by UMP. This form allows you to consent to personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage your options.
  • Before requesting ads, the app checks the consent status returned by UMP. Ads may be requested when consent status is Not Required or Obtained. If you do not provide required consent, only non-personalized or no ads may be served as determined by Google.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the app's privacy settings option (for example, a "Privacy Options" or "Privacy Settings" entry) where available, which re-displays the UMP consent form. You can also manage ad personalization in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads, and by resetting your advertising ID.

Consent signals collected through UMP are shared with Google and its advertising partners in accordance with Google's requirements and applicable regulations. We do not store your UMP consent choices on our own servers.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Third-party analytics: Based on the current build reviewed, the app does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and any required disclosures will be added.

Local crash handling: The app includes a local crash handling mechanism that saves a crash log file (e.g., last crash log) to the app's private cache directory on your device if an unexpected error occurs. This file remains on your device and is not automatically transmitted to us. The app may offer you an optional bug reporting screen where you can review the captured log and, if you choose, include it in a report you send to us via email or other support channel you initiate. Sending such a report is voluntary and performed by you.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only as follows:

  • With Google AdMob and Google Play Services: To provide advertising, to check consent information, and to ensure proper app functionality. Sharing is governed by Google's policies.
  • With your own actions: When you use Android system sharing to share an .lrc file, or when you voluntarily send a bug report that includes device and crash information.
  • For legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or to comply with Google Play policies, we may disclose information as required.

8. Data Retention

  • Locally stored lyrics and preferences: Retained on your device until you delete the files, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Crash logs in cache: Retained in app cache until cleared by you, by the system, or upon app uninstall.
  • Advertising data: Retention is controlled by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's retention policies. We do not retain advertising data on our own servers.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid collecting unnecessary personal data. Lyrics files and audio access are handled locally on your device, so the security of that content depends in part on your device security (lock screen, encryption, permissions). Advertising data is transmitted over secure connections by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Deletion

Because most content is stored locally:

  • You can delete individual .lrc files using the app or your file manager.
  • You can clear the app's cache and data via Android Settings > Apps > LRC-Maker & Editor > Storage > Clear Cache / Clear Data.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove all app-private data.
  • For data collected by Google AdMob, you may manage or delete it via your Google account, reset your advertising ID, or opt-out of personalized ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. You may also use the privacy options provided through UMP in the app.

If you contact us for data deletion assistance, please include details at clouddream.app@gmail.com.
